## Scientific and provenance assessment

**Evidence position association: design PASS with explicit clarification.** Optional Evidence.residue_refs names an actual molecule chain/raw index through that Evidence's own direct subject path, including Material → Construct → Molecule. It never borrows a sequence, parent/child map or same-named chain from another candidate. It must be labelled **Source-reported position**, retaining the actual Evidence kind, whole-subject value/unit, assay conditions, source/version and time.

An anchor does not prove that a whole-Material measured scalar was experimentally localized to that residue. A linked position is not automatically a prediction or a scientific concern classification. Optional description retains source qualitative text; it is not classified from its words, hidden in assay conditions, or converted into a fake number. A local research explanation remains an annotation or proposed Hypothesis according to its actual provenance. These clarifications were explicitly accepted by the implementation agent during review.

**Exact structural observation: design PASS.** The optional Hypothesis structural residue contains the full actual source residue key and supplied alternate identifier under a named immutable Structure/model context. It must resolve against that source, model and conformer again at preview and import. Null/unknown identifiers remain distinct from blank/not-applicable. A source-only antigen, ligand or unmapped residue can be retained as an observed coordinate context without creating an antibody sequence position, Material measurement attribution or implied correspondence. Existing model/map consistency remains mandatory.

UI persistence retains opaque source/view identifiers and actual alternate codes only. Full source keys are resolved from local immutable data for the reviewed saved Hypothesis; coordinates, residue names, scientific note drafts and property values do not enter localStorage. A stale UID from another source/model/project cannot be silently rebound by residue number or proximity.

**Rendering: design PASS for the stated primitive scope.** Exact source atoms remain authoritative. Existing locally pinned 3Dmol styles and zoom-to-selection operations provide the needed display/focus behavior. Native assignBonds is documented as a primitive coordinate-distance operation producing single bonds without valence enforcement. Ball-and-stick must therefore be identified as geometry-based rendering, not validated connectivity, bond order, interaction energy or evidence. Unknown elements/alternates remain visible but unconnected; incompatible alternate states must not acquire spurious connections. No calculated bond replaces or modifies stored coordinate source data. [3Dmol GLModel](https://3dmol.org/doc/GLModel.html)

C-alpha overview is a subset of actual source atoms of standard amino-acid residues, with selected-residue atomic detail. It is not a generated backbone, continuous trace, secondary-structure assignment or cartoon. Missing C-alpha atoms are not interpolated; calcium ions and modified/unknown residues must not become C-alpha protein positions through a name-only guess. The original complete atom mode remains available.

Focus changes the camera using exactly the selected existing source atoms; it does not choose a nearest residue or create a mapping. Mode/focus changes must preserve source/model/conformer identity and the difference between mapped sequence and source-only selection. Linked view orientation/zoom retains independent centers and does not superpose coordinate frames. Existing documentation mentioning a viewer-generated cartoon should be aligned with the actual implemented modes rather than retaining an unsupported cartoon claim.

## Public experimental scale fixture

[RCSB 1MLC](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1MLC) is a suitable public experimental scale input: an antibody Fab/lysozyme complex entry determined by X-ray diffraction at 2.50 Å, entry version 1.3 dated 2024-10-23. The page reports 8,792 atoms and 1,122 modeled/deposited polymer residues. It contains six protein chain instances: light chains A/C, heavy chains B/D and lysozyme E/F, representing three unique protein entities. Its asymmetric-unit copies must not be mistaken for independent candidate predictions or collapsed by identical sequence.

The entry page establishes the candidate and reported metadata, not the exact bytes or complete parser counts of a yet-to-be-pinned local input. Preserve an unmodified coordinate download with URL, revision, exact SHA-256 and retrieval record. Count the actual local parsed atoms and all source residues separately from the webpage's polymer-residue statistic; water/ligand residues may also be retained. Use actual deposited entity/chain/label-sequence fields for explicit mapping and retain all source instances. Do not generate an IMGT assignment from this file or invoke automatic label/sequence assignment as if deposited.

The [wwPDB usage policy](https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies) makes PDB archive data available under CC0 and encourages attribution to original depositors. Record this data provenance separately from application/dependency licensing. Runtime and acceptance tests use checked-in local input, with no on-demand external download or private data transmission.

1MLC must retain experimental origin. It supplies neither ipTM/pLDDT nor new predictive confidence, and B-values or crystallographic R-values cannot become model confidence. It can verify meaningful scale, source preservation and actual picking, not prediction accuracy, human-validated scientific goldens or actual researcher usefulness. The separate clearly labelled synthetic prediction/assay fixtures are still needed for the exact J03/J04 engineering paths and negatives.

## Independently proposed required boundaries

1. **Direct subject versus positional implication.** Two equal-sequence candidates have identical chain names but different Materials. Accept only the Evidence's own molecule chain/index; reject the other's chain, out-of-range index or duplicate anchor atomically. Retain a null measurement as null and zero as zero. A measured whole-sample scalar with a position link stays a whole-subject value, and a qualitative description remains raw source text without a generated score/classification. Malicious note markup must display as text.
2. **Structural source/model/conformer isolation.** Attempt the same author number on another chain, model, source or alternate; null versus blank identifiers; microheterogeneity with a different residue name; a correspondence from another model; and a genuine unknown model. Retain the exact actual source observation or reject; do not choose an integer neighbour, first alternate/model or sequence analogue. Source-only antigen/unmapped selections must create no antibody raw-position or Material-measurement claim.
3. **Incompatible geometry and unknown source atoms.** Place A/B alternatives close enough to satisfy the renderer's distance threshold and include blank common atoms, unknown alt codes and unknown elements. Verify mutually exclusive/unknown states do not acquire spurious bonds, common atoms are handled only under the declared display policy, and all original atoms/fields remain available. Stored text/hash/export cannot acquire renderer-generated bonds or altered coordinates.
4. **Overview picking and missing atoms.** Include a calcium ion named CA, a standard residue lacking CA, nonstandard/modified residues, and a selected antigen/water/ligand. Only actual eligible C-alpha source atoms form the overview, with selected-source atomic detail as declared. No pseudo-atom or secondary structure appears. Hidden atoms must not steal physical picks; visible overview/detail picks must resolve to their actual source indices.
5. **Focus and stale-context lifecycle.** Select a mapped residue, then an unavailable sequence coordinate; focus must not reuse the old structural selection. Change model/map/project or representation while a view/source resolution or Hypothesis preview/save is pending. Preserve current state and reject obsolete work, retaining the F10/F11 draft-isolation guarantees. Linked focus/view changes must preserve independent source centers rather than imply superposition.
6. **Legacy and immutable snapshots.** Export/reimport old Evidence and Hypotheses without optional fields byte-for-byte at the record level; absent fields remain absent. New optional fields must validate without backfilling old records or modifying frozen Decision Evidence snapshots. Source anchors remain on the original parent Evidence after a child is made; no child measured data or mapped numbering is inherited.
7. **Real scale and offline provenance.** Verify local 1MLC SHA and actual source/parsed counts, all six chain instances, all atoms including non-polymers, original fields, physical selection/focus and usable representation changes. Block external requests during replay. Preserve experimental origin and missing predictive confidence; no B/R-value confidence substitute and no automatic assembly transformation. Record any limit or unsupported source field rather than silently dropping it.

The required named paths remain `e2e_j03_complex_comparison`, `e2e_j03_unmappable_residue`, `e2e_j03_missing_confidence`, `e2e_j04_developability_review`, `e2e_j04_unit_mismatch` and `e2e_j04_unknown_assay_condition`. Each complete main action must save the intended source-bearing hypothesis/design and survive an actual process restart and export/reimport. J04 must show measured/computed separation and conditions, make the actual numbered child and preserve original Material attribution. Physical canvas interaction, reciprocal selection and camera state must be independently checked; direct calls to selection helpers are insufficient.
